Canyon & Lakes: day by day

Day 1

Leave Almaty at 7:00. Walk the Valley of Castles in Charyn Canyon. Night in a guesthouse in Saty village.

Day 2

Hike to the first and second Kolsai lakes. Picnic lunch by the water. Evening by the fire.

Day 3

Morning at Lake Kaindy and its sunken forest. Return to Almaty by 20:00.

What to bring

  • Comfortable hiking shoes
  • Warm jacket and rain cover
  • Sunglasses and a hat
  • ID card or passport

Not included

  • Flights or trains to Almaty and Astana
  • Travel insurance
  • Personal expenses and souvenirs
