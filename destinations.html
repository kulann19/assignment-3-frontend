Destinations

Six places we know best, from the red Charyn Canyon to the ancient city of Turkistan.

A 154 km canyon of red sandstone. Walk the Valley of Castles down to the Charyn River.

Akmola Region

Burabay

Pine forests, clear lakes and granite rocks. Often called the Kazakh Switzerland.

2 days 240 km from Astana
Turkistan Region

Turkistan

An ancient Silk Road city and home of the Khoja Ahmed Yasawi mausoleum, a UNESCO site.

2 days Train from Astana

Best time to visit

  • May – June: green steppe, wild tulips, mild weather.
  • July – August: best for mountain lakes and hiking.
  • September – October: golden autumn and fewer tourists.
  • Winter: ski resorts near Almaty and frozen lakes.

Travel tips

  1. Bring layers: mountain weather changes fast.
  2. Carry cash for small villages and national park fees.
  3. Use sunscreen even on cloudy days at high altitude.
  4. Learn a few words: Salemetsiz be means “Hello”.
